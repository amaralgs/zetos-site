About Zetos

Zetos is a virtual personal trainer for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It started from a simple wish: to know how to do each exercise correctly, without having to guess, and to keep sets, rests and loads in one place.

The demonstrations are original illustrations and short videos, shown from three angles, with the points to watch for. The content is a draft under professional review; where something has been reviewed, the app says so.

Zetos runs on your device. Your profile, history and recordings stay with you.
