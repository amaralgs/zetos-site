Features

Clear, calm, at your pace.

Zetos shows how to do each exercise, keeps your sets and rests organised, and stays out of your way.

🎬

Looping demonstrations

Three views of every movement, with pause and restart. Original illustrations, no stock footage.

🧭

What to watch for

Setup, range of motion, breathing and the mistakes to avoid, in plain language.

⏱️

Sets and rests

Start and finish each set, log load, reps and effort. The rest timer survives closing the app.

🏋️

Your gym, your gear

Save more than one gym and swap an exercise for another with the same movement pattern.

🎥

Record and compare

Film your set, watch it in slow motion and compare it with the reference. No automatic judging.

🔒

Private by default

Your data stays on your device. Health data is optional and never sent to the chat.

Apple Watch

Your wrist keeps the pace.

The companion app shows rest alerts and, when you allow it, monitors heart rate and saves the session to Health.

  • Rest-end haptic alerts
  • Heart rate during the workout, only when the watch is connected
  • Session saved to Apple Health with your permission

Work in progress

Zetos is a prototype. The exercise content is still being reviewed by a fitness professional, and the app does not replace one. Talk to a professional before starting a new programme.

Privacy

We store none of your information.

The developer of Zetos does not store absolutely any user information: no account, no sign-up, no data server, no ads, no tracking. Your profile, history and recordings stay only on your device.

No account

Nothing to create, nothing to sign in to.

Everything on your device

Profile, workouts and videos never leave it.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics, no ad networks.

You are in control

Delete a recording or uninstall the app and everything is gone.

Optional coach chat: if you choose to send a question, its text is forwarded to an AI provider to produce the answer. The developer does not store it, and your profile, Health data and videos are never included.
