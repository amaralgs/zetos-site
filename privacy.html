Zetos – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Developer: Gilberto Amaral · Draft, pending legal review

The developer does not store absolutely any user information.

No account, no data server, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device. The only exception is the optional coach chat, described below.

In short

Zetos keeps your profile, workout history and recordings on your device. There is no account. The developer does not receive your data. The only thing that can leave your device is the text of a question you choose to send to the optional coach chat.

What the app stores

Goals, schedule preferences, gyms and equipment, workout sessions (sets, loads, repetitions, effort) and metadata of your recordings. All of it stays on your device.

Camera and recordings

The camera is used only when you choose to record a set. Videos stay on your device, and you can delete or export them. There is no automatic analysis.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

If you allow it, Zetos reads height, weight and body-fat percentage to fill in your profile, and the Apple Watch app reads heart rate during a workout and saves the session to Health. You can change these permissions at any time in iOS settings. Health data is not included in the coach chat.

Coach chat (optional)

When you use the chat, the text of your question is sent to a server configured by the developer, which forwards it to an AI provider (Anthropic or Google) and returns the answer. Your profile, Health data and videos are not included. The server does not log questions or answers.

Advertising and tracking

Zetos has no advertising and no analytics, and does not track you.

Children

Zetos is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights

Because the data stays on your device, you control it: delete a recording, clear history, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

Not medical advice

Zetos is not a medical device or a licensed professional. Consult a professional before starting a new exercise programme.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated.
